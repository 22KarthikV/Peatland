**Method:**
- 5 km blocks aligned to the CRS origin (block ids do not depend on the raster). Blocks touched by any patch are assigned; blocks that intersect the demo box are forced to `test`; the rest are shuffled with `seed` so overall block counts approach 60/20/20.
- A patch takes the split S of the block holding its centre. It is kept only if every block it touches is S and no block within `buffer` has a higher-priority split (test > val > train). Otherwise it is dropped (`None`). The buffer strip comes out of the lower-priority side, so small val/test blocks are not emptied.
- Guarantee: kept patches in different splits are at least `buffer` apart (no overlap, no touching neighbours when buffer > 0). Train and val patches are at least `buffer` from the demo box. Every patch inside the demo box is kept as `test`.

**Gotchas:**
- With 256 px (2.56 km) patches in 5 km blocks, many patches straddle a block edge and are dropped: on a synthetic grid around the demo site about 45% drop at buffer 0 and about 65% at 1 km (kept: train ~0.2, val ~0.05, test ~0.1 of all patches; test is inflated by the forced demo blocks). A denser stride keeps the drop ratio but covers more of each block. Lower `buffer` or larger `block_size` raise yield if M4/M5 run short of val data.
- Block fractions are counted over all touched blocks, including forced test blocks, so the test share of blocks is the larger of 20% and the demo footprint.
- The CRS must be projected (metres); EPSG:4326 raises `ValueError`.
